The Case, part 2

This morning, Mr. Novak arrived at the gallery at 9 AM. He went to check on "The Midnight Moon", but it wasn't on the wall. He panicked. He remembered the blackout and immediately thought that a thief used the darkness to steal the painting.

Mr. Novak was too embarrassed to call the police, so he called his friend instead: the famous Detective Lance. Now Lance needs a witness who can answer the question: what exactly happened that night?

Grammar

Past Simple — the tense of finished actions

We use the Past Simple for actions that started and finished at a specific point in the past. It's how Mr. Novak explains exactly what he did yesterday — and how we're going to work out what really happened last night.

Part 1 — "to be": was / were

Affirmative Negative Question
I / he / she / it was wasn't Was I/he/she/it ...?
you / we / they were weren't Were you/we/they ...?
  • (+) Beatrice was at the gallery last night.
  • (+) The lights were off during the blackout.
  • (-) Oliver wasn't awake at midnight.
  • (-) The cameras weren't working.
  • (?) Was the alarm broken?
  • (?) Were you at the gallery last night?

Part 3 — Time expressions

Past Simple almost always comes with a time expression that tells us exactly when something happened:

yesterday · last night / week / month · two days ago · at 11:30 PM · this morning

They usually go at the end of the sentence, or at the beginning for emphasis:

  • Mr. Novak arrived at 09:00 AM.
  • Yesterday, Mr. Novak was not in the gallery.
  • Beatrice left the workshop two hours ago.
  • Last night, nobody heard anything unusual.

Part 4 — Wh-questions

  • Wh-words: who, what, where, when, why, how…
  • a) with "to be": Wh-word + was/were + subject?
    • Where was Mr. Novak last night?
    • Who were the suspects?
  • b) with verbs: Wh-word + did + subject + base verb?
    • What did Oliver see?
    • Why did the cameras break?
  • c) subject questions — no "did"! When who or what is the one doing the action, we use the past tense verb directly, just like with he/she/it in the present:
    • Who took the painting? (NOT "Who did take the painting?")
    • What happened at midnight? (NOT "What did happen at midnight?")

Homework

Was/Were vs. Did в питальних та заперечних реченнях

Коли ми будуємо питання або заперечення в Past Simple, нам потрібен допоміжний елемент. Що саме обрати – did чи was/were? Запитай себе: "Чи є у цьому реченні дія, яку хтось виконує, чи лише опис стану?"

  • Є дія (Action verbs): використовуємо did / didn't. Якщо ми говоримо про дію (buy, go, check, explain), нам потрібне did + базова форма дієслова (без закінчення -ed чи другої форми).
  • Немає дії (Дієслово "to be"): використовуємо was / were. Якщо ми описуємо, ким/яким щось було (іменник, прикметник) або де щось знаходилось, то використовуємо форми минулого часу від to be, без did.

Wh- questions (Спеціальні питання)

Якщо потрібно додати питальне слово (Why, Where, When, What time, How), воно просто ставиться на самий початок речення, перед did або was/were:

  • Why did she buy a ticket? (Дія)
  • Why was the ticket expensive? (Стан / опис)
  • Where did they go? (Дія)
  • Where were they? (Місце)

Practice

Exercise 1: Ask the Question

Перетвори ці стверджувальні (+) речення на питання (?). Якщо в дужках є питальне слово, побудуй спеціальне питання. Спочатку виріши: тут потрібне was/were чи did?

Examples:
Sofia waited in line. → Did Sofia wait in line?
She waited in line. (Why) → Why did she wait in line?
The line was long. (Why) → Why was the line long?
