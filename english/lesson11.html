Post-It Notes

Коли ми говоримо про незлічувані продукти або множину злічуваних, ми рідко знаємо точну кількість. Щоб сказати "трохи" або "кілька", ми використовуємо слова some та any.

Some використовується в стверджувальних реченнях:

  • I have some mushrooms. — У мене є кілька грибів.
  • There is some juice in the glass. — У склянці є трохи соку.

Any використовується в запереченнях та звичайних запитаннях:

  • Do you have any strawberries? — У тебе є (хоч якісь) полуниці?
  • There isn't any sugar in my tea. — У моєму чаї немає (ніякого) цукру.

The Failed Glue That Became a Bestseller

In 1968, Spencer Silver worked as a scientist at a company called 3M in the US. Every day, he was testing new kinds of glue in his laboratory. His goal was simple: to invent the strongest glue in the world.

One day, Silver was mixing different chemicals, as usual. He was hoping this new combination would make a strong, powerful glue. But something went wrong. The result was the opposite: instead of a strong glue, he got a very weak one. It didn't stick well at all. You could pull it off paper very easily, and it didn't leave any mark.

At first, everyone at the company thought this weak glue was useless. For six long years, Silver talked to his colleagues about his strange invention because he believed it was special. But nobody else understood it.

In 1974, another man worked at the same company. His name was Art Fry. Fry was singing in his church choir every Sunday. He was also using small pieces of paper to mark the pages of his songbook. But there was a problem: the papers always fell out of the book.

One boring afternoon, Fry was thinking about his songbook problem. Suddenly, he remembered Silver's weak glue. He knew immediately: this strange glue could hold paper in place, and it wouldn’t damage the paper at all! Fry was excited. He tested small pieces of paper with Silver's glue. Step by step, he improved his idea.

By 1980, 3M was selling this new invention all over the United States. Today, we call this invention the “Post-it Note”, and people all around the world use billions of these notes every year. And it all started with a "failed" glue…
