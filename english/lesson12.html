The Case

Usually, the City Gallery is a quiet place. Every morning, Mr. Novak, the director, unlocks the doors. The famous painting, “The Midnight Moon”, hangs in the main hall. People from all over the world visit the gallery to see this masterpiece.

Several people work in the gallery. Mia and Sam are the cleaners. They come to work early in the morning to clean the building before the visitors arrive. Oliver is the night guard. He walks around the gallery every hour. He always drinks coffee, and he never naps at work – or so he says. In a small workshop behind the main hall, Beatrice repairs old and damaged frames. She is the restorer. Her routine is always the same: she arrives in the afternoon and leaves late at night.

But today something is wrong. Last night there was a blackout in the gallery, and when the lights came back on, "The Midnight Moon" was gone. The famous Detective Lance arrives to investigate. Everyone who works in the gallery is a suspect. Where is the painting now?

Grammar

Present Simple — the tense of facts and routines

We use the Present Simple when something is generally true or happens repeatedly as part of a routine. It's the most common tense in everyday English, and today it's our main tool: before we can spot what's unusual at the gallery, we first need to describe what's normal.

Part 1 — "to be" + nouns/adjectives

Affirmative Negative Question
I am am not Am I ...?
he / she / it is isn't Is he/she/it ...?
you / we / they are aren't Are you/we/they ...?
  • (+) Oliver is the night guard.
  • (+) The paintings are very valuable.
  • (−) Mia isn't in the main hall.
  • (−) The gallery isn't open on Mondays.
  • (?) Is Mr. Novak the director?
  • (?) Are the cameras new?

Part 3 — Frequency words

always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never

завжди → зазвичай → часто → іноді → рідко → ніколи

They go before the main verb, but after "to be":

  • Oliver always drinks coffee on his shift.
  • Mia never leaves a mess behind.
  • Sam is rarely in the main hall.
  • The workers are always polite to the visitors.

Part 4 — Wh-questions

  • Wh-words: who, what, where, when, why, how, what time, how often…
  • a) with "to be": Wh-word + am/is/are + subject?
    • Who is the main suspect?
    • Where are the cameras?
    • Why isn't the gallery open on Monday?
  • b) with verbs: Wh-word + do/does + subject + base verb?
    • What does Oliver drink?
    • Where do Mia and Sam work?
  • c) frequency adverbs inside the question: the adverb slots in between the subject and the verb, same spot as always.
    • How is Beatrice always so focused?
    • Why does Oliver always drink coffee?
